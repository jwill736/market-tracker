Alert scorecard

Every alert, bought at the first close after it went out and measured against SPY over the same days at 1, 3 and 6 months. It only claims an edge once enough alerts have 3 months behind them.

Receipts

Every early-wire call, with the price when it was first seen, published 24 hours later and scored 5 trading days on against SPY. Misses stay in. Each finished day is sealed with a SHA-256 hash chained to the day before, stored in this repository's journal-data branch, so no call can be changed or dropped afterwards without breaking every later seal.
