                Taxes across your accounts

                Robinhood can't see your Stash buys and Stash can't see Coinbase, so no broker warns you about a wash sale across them: selling at a loss and buying the same thing (or a fund tracking the same index) within 30 days either side cancels the deduction. Not tax advice; an IRA or a spouse's account counts too and isn't visible here.

                Before December 31

                Losses you take by the last trading day of the year offset this year's gains, then up to $3,000 of other income; the rest carries forward. If your income is low, long-term gains can be taken at a 0% federal rate. Estimates at your rates, not tax advice.

                Your tax situation (makes the numbers yours)

                Taxable income is after the standard deduction (last year's return, line 15, is a good starting guess). The 0% limit defaults to the 2026 IRS figure for your filing status; it changes every year, so check it.

                Income

                Dividends you've been paid (from your Robinhood file; estimated from ex-dates and your shares where an account has no dividend rows, like Stash), what your holdings pay a year at today's rate, and when the next ones land.                                                                   Did the app's advice beat just buying VOO?

                                                                  Every day the hold plan's sells, trims and buys are written down with that day's price. Each is then scored against what you'd have done anyway: put the money in VOO. A sell helped if the stock then did worse than VOO; a buy helped if it did better.

                                                                  Does the score actually work?

                                                                  Each day's scores are logged, then compared with what prices did 1, 3 and 6 months later. IC is the rank correlation between score and later return: 0 means no edge, +0.05 is a small real edge, +0.10 is strong. A scheduled GitHub Actions job records the built-in universe every weekday; mt journal sync pulls it here.
