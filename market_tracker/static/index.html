                                      Import your accounts

                                      In the Robinhood app: Account → Reports and statements → Reports → generate an account activity CSV for all dates, then choose it here. Buys, sells and splits are imported; dividends, transfers and options are listed and skipped. Robinhood Crypto isn't in that file: add coins with Stash / other.

                                      On coinbase.com: Profile → Statements (or Taxes → Documents) → generate a transaction history CSV for all time. Buys, sells, conversions and rewards (at their value when received) are imported; sends, receives and deposits are listed and skipped.

                                      Stash has no trade export, so type each holding once: SYMBOL SHARES TOTAL_COST [DATE], one per line, e.g. VOO 3.214 1450.20 2024-03-01. Shares and cost basis are on your statement or under each investment in the app. Add the purchase date if you know it, or taxes will treat every gain as short-term.

                                      Does the score actually work?

                                      Each day's scores are logged, then compared with what prices did 1, 3 and 6 months later. IC is the rank correlation between score and later return: 0 means no edge, +0.05 is a small real edge, +0.10 is strong. A scheduled GitHub Actions job records the built-in universe every weekday; mt journal sync pulls it here.
